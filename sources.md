# Research Sources

- [Evolving Virtual Creatures](https://doi.org/10.1145/192161.192167) — Graph-based morphology and controller evolution can produce varied locomotion, but outcomes strongly depend on behavioral evaluation.
- [A Fast and Elitist Multiobjective Genetic Algorithm: NSGA-II](https://doi.org/10.1109/4235.996017) — Non-dominated sorting and crowding preserve competing objectives without collapsing them into one fragile weighted score.
- [Illuminating Search Spaces by Mapping Elites](https://arxiv.org/abs/1504.04909) — MAP-Elites preserves high-performing solutions across explicitly chosen behavioral niches.
- [Abandoning Objectives: Evolution Through the Search for Novelty Alone](https://doi.org/10.1162/EVCO_a_00025) — Behavioral novelty can escape deceptive local optima that objective-only evolution reinforces.
- [Quality and Diversity in Evolutionary Modular Robotics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2008.02116) — Quality-diversity search is effective for maintaining high-performing repertoires across varied robot morphologies.
- [Co-optimizing for Task Performance and Energy Efficiency in Evolvable Robots](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.engappai.2022.104968) — Energy-aware multi-objective evolution can improve gait performance and convergence rather than merely slowing creatures down.
- [Sim-to-Real Learning of All Common Bipedal Gaits via Periodic Reward Composition](https://arxiv.org/abs/2011.01387) — Contact timing and periodic composition provide controllable gait descriptions without relying solely on forward velocity.
- [Evolution of Central Pattern Generators for a Five-Link Biped](https://arxiv.org/abs/0801.0830) — Evolved coupled oscillators are well suited to generating diverse coordinated legged locomotion.
- [Controller Architectures for Arbitrary Robot Morphologies](https://doi.org/10.1109/SSCI52147.2023.10371941) — Morphology-independent locomotion benefits from controllers that exploit structural information rather than fixed body-specific wiring.
